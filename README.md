A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty ($500, top 50 share it).

## The joke

GTA 6 is finally out after 13 years of waiting since GTA V — so the picture is the iconic GTA death screen: **WASTED — 13 YEARS OF WAITING**. But the make.fun ghost, standing in a Vice City sunset with its star badge, did not wait: its agent got paid on make.fun. The program draws the whole scene itself — sunset gradient, retro sun, palm silhouettes, the ghost, and the captions in a hand-designed 5x7 bitmap font — with a tiny software rasterizer and a minimal PNG encoder, standard library only.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/meme.png` is the meme (1200x675). When the post is live, `POST.json` at the repository root names its address on X and the program copies it to `out/POST.json` as well.
